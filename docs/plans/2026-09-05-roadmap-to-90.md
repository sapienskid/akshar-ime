# Roadmap to 90% — a rethink of how every byte of data should work

Date: 2026-09-05, post-cleanup. Supersedes the accuracy ordering in
`2026-09-05-research-agenda.md` (which is paper-oriented). Current shipped
state: **80.98% native top-1 / 60.64% ALL**, decode 14 ms, oracle top-50
native **94.40%**.

---

## 1. How the data is used today (exhaustive audit)

**Aksharantar `train_devanagari.jsonl` (3.59M pairs):**
- Emissions `P(roman chunk | akshara)` — 12 EM iterations over the pairs
  (`em_trainer.rs`), the model's only knowledge of *how users spell*.
- Akshara KN trigram LM — counted deterministically from the Devanagari side.
  The model's only knowledge of *word shape*.
- That is ALL. The lexicon mined from it (`build_lexicon`) is not shipped
  (`roman_lexicon.bin` absent), so at runtime Aksharantar contributes nothing
  beyond the two tables baked into `translit_model.bin`.

**`store/corpus_clean.txt` (86.1M tokens):**
- Word unigram counts → `word_freq_text.bin` (470k words) → a single
  log-linear rescoring term `−λ·ln(1+f)` over the decoder's k=50 list.
- That is ALL. `word_pairs.csv` (6.5M bigrams, E6 context) is unused; the
  character-level statistics of 86M tokens are unused; the corpus's coverage
  of *which* words exist is used only implicitly through the rescoring.

**Structural observation:** the corpus is worth +5 points as a unigram table,
but the model itself has never seen a single byte of it. Everything the model
knows about Nepali spelling comes from 3.6M annotation pairs. That is the
asymmetry to attack.

## 2. Where the 9 points live

Decomposition of the native gap (oracle top-50 94.40 vs top-1 80.98):

- **13.4 points of ranking error** — gold is generated but ranked below a
  confusable. 52% of misses are matra-only real-word pairs (कल/काल) where the
  roman string carries zero bits; the *only* separating signals are priors.
- **5.6 points of generation error** — gold never enters the beam (oracle
  ceiling). Named entities are worst (NEI top-50 80.53).
- ALL-bucket note: the NE ranking gap is ~34 points — candidates exist, rank
  is wrong. Frequency can't fix NE (rare words); shape and context can.

Any credible path to 90 must (a) raise the oracle and (b) close most of the
ranking gap. Neither hand-tuned additive fusion (measured failure) nor more
self-training (measured collapse) does that.

## 3. The program (ordered, with the information each step adds)

### S1 — Leakage-free discriminative reranker (target +4–6)

Replace the single λ with trained weights over the k=50 list. Features:
decoder cost, **shaped frequency** (rank and log, interpolated with discount
smoothing — not raw ln(1+f)), word length in aksharas and in roman, matra
profile (counts of ा/ि/े/ो… — the matra-confusable discriminator),
conjunct count, final-akshara identity, boundary-emission costs, in-vocab
flag, and **suffix prior**: Nepali postpositions (को/का/की/लाई/ले/मा/बाट/हरू)
are romanized glued to the stem; a candidate whose final aksharas spell a
common postposition while the roman ends in ko/lai/le/ma/baat/haru earns a
prior. Train by logistic loss on valid (+train-derived negatives: for a
sample of pairs, the other k-best members); tune thresholds on valid only;
single-shot test. This is the "different statistical tool" with the best
effort/points ratio — the k-best lists and features are already computed
piecemeal.

### S2 — Exact vocabulary scoring: kill the generation ceiling (target oracle → ~97, top-1 +1–3)

The beam-limited k=50 oracle (94.4) is an artifact of generation, not of
information. The word-trie lattice intersection (`decode_in_words`, already
built) can score **every** vocabulary word exactly — the true noisy channel
`P(D)·Σ_σ Π P(s|a)` with the alignment sum computed by forward pass inside
the trie walk, replacing beam truncation. First attempt (M4 graph layer)
failed because it fused additively with hand weights and pre-dates the clean
vocab; redo it as the *candidate source feeding S1's reranker* (trie-exact
score is just another feature), not as a competing ranker.

### S3 — Conditional lattice model (v3 CRF): the principled tool change (target +1–3, removes hand constants)

The v2 lesson (additive fusion injects EM-diluted bias) points at the fix:
train a **conditional** model over the lattice — a sparse-feature CRF whose
edge features are (chunk, akshara, prev chunk, prev akshara, position class)
with L-BFGS on the conditional likelihood of gold paths. Unlike the EM
pair-grammar, a CRF conditions on the observed roman, so the ना/न posterior
bias cannot form; unlike hand λ's, all weights are jointly estimated. Keep
the generative model as the feature base; the CRF re-weights its edges.
This is also the honest "different statistical tool" the project identity
(no neural net, ~ms latency) permits.

### S4 — E6 word-bigram context (product lever, +2–4 effective)

Unchanged from the research agenda: conditional prior P(w_i|w_{i−1}) from
`word_pairs.csv`, sentence harness, Kirov-calibrated. Does not move the
isolated-word benchmark; moves real-user accuracy past anything the
benchmark can show.

### S5 — Multi-reference evaluation mode (measurement integrity)

IndicXlit counts a hit against up to 4 romanized variants; we score against
one. Until we mirror that protocol, every comparison in either direction is
approximate. Cheap to build (`evaluate_model --multi-ref`), needed before
claiming any number crosses anything.

### Sequencing

S5 + S1 first (measurement + biggest ratio), then S2, then S3; S4 in
parallel whenever sentence-level eval exists. Honest ceiling estimate:
S1+S2 put native top-1 in the 86–89 range; 90 requires S3 as well, and even
then residual matra pairs with equal priors are irreducible from the string
alone. 90% *effective* (context-aware) accuracy is the more defensible
target; the benchmark's 90 is the stretch goal this program is built to
attempt.

## 4. What we deliberately do NOT do

- Neural sequence models: violates the project's identity (no NN, ms latency,
  9 MB WASM) and the classical stack is measurably competitive (80.98 vs
  IndicXlit 80.25 without re-ranking).
- More self-training / synthetic pairs: falsified (B: −1.1, C: −5.0).
- Hand-tuned additive fusion of new signals: falsified (v2, M4-pilot).
